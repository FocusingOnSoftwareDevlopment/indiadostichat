DUNO dragon clash card game Rules

  • Clans Play: Play cards matching the current active Clan (Fire, Ice, Forest, Thunder) or rank number.
  • Dragon Freeze (Skip): Pauses the next player's turn.
  • Dragon Turn (Reverse): Reverses the direction of play.
  • Dragon Bite +2 (Draw Two): Forces the next player to draw 2 cards and skips their turn.
  • Elder Dragon (Wild): Allows changing the current active Clan.
  • Chaos Dragon +4 (Wild Draw 4): Allows changing the active Clan and forces the next player to draw 4 cards.
  • ROAR: Press the ROAR button when you have exactly 1 card left. Failure to roar before others notice results in drawing 2 penalty cards.
